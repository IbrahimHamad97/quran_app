Privacy Policy

Quran & Tafsir · Last updated: 23 October 2026

This privacy policy describes how the Quran & Tafsir mobile app (“the App”) handles information.

Summary

The App works offline. It does not require an account and does not collect personal data, analytics, advertising identifiers, or location.

Data we collect

We do not collect personal information. There are no backend servers operated by us for this App, and the App does not send your data to us.

Data stored on your device

The following may be stored locally on your device only:

  • Reading progress (last position)
  • Bookmarks and notes
  • App settings (for example theme preference)

This information stays on your device. Uninstalling the App removes it (subject to how your device manages app storage).

Internet access

After installation, Quran text and tafsir content are available offline from data bundled with the App. The App is not designed to upload your bookmarks, notes, or reading history.

Advertising and tracking

The App does not show ads and does not use an advertising ID for tracking.

Children

The App does not knowingly collect personal data from children. Content is religious reading material; no personal information is requested.

Third-party services

Distribution through Google Play is subject to Google’s own terms and privacy policy. We do not integrate third-party analytics or crash SDKs in the App.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the “Last updated” date.
